## Part 2 — AI in Saving & Emergency Fund Strategy

AI tools now automate saving by:
- Analysing cashflow volatility  
- Identifying low‑risk saving opportunities  
- Moving small amounts into savings automatically  
- Calculating “safe‑to-save” values  

### Formula: AI‑Adjusted Safe‑to‑Save
`STS = (Average Surplus × Stability Score) − Volatility Index`

Where:
- Stability Score = 0.4–1.0  
- Volatility Index = based on month‑to‑month fluctuations  

This ensures you never save too aggressively.
