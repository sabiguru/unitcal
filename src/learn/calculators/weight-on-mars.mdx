# What Would You Weigh on Mars?

Stepping onto Mars, you'd instantly feel lighter — not because you've changed, but because **gravity** has. This guide explains the difference between **mass** and **weight**, why Mars's gravity is ~38% of Earth's, and how to convert your Earth weight to a Martian one accurately. Use this to make sense of the number you'll see in our tool — and what it means for real life on the Red Planet.

## Weight vs Mass (The One-Liner You'll Remember)

- **Mass**: how much "stuff" you're made of — it doesn't change with location
- **Weight**: gravitational **force** on that mass — it depends on the planet

On Earth, surface gravity is about **9.81 m/s²**. On Mars, it's **3.71 m/s²** — roughly **0.38× Earth**.

That ratio lets you convert instantly:

**`Weight on Mars = Weight on Earth × 0.38`**

If you prefer working from mass:

**`Weight (N) = Mass (kg) × g (m/s²)`**
- Earth: `W = m × 9.81`
- Mars: `W = m × 3.71`

Our calculator uses the above ratio for a quick, intuitive number.

## Why Mars Pulls Less

Gravity at a planet's surface depends on **mass** and **radius**:

**g = (G × M) / r^2**

Where:
- **G** = gravitational constant
- **M** = planet's mass
- **r** = planet's radius

Mars has ~**11%** of Earth's mass and about **53%** of Earth's radius. Smaller radius concentrates gravity, but the much lower mass dominates, yielding ~**0.38 g_Earth**

## What Lower Gravity Does to You

- **Motion**: jumps feel springy; sprints have longer hang-time
- **Bones/Muscles**: long-term deconditioning without countermeasures (think ISS lessons)
- **Posture**: slight spinal decompression; you may stand a tad taller
- **Daily life**: loads, tools, and safety standards must be rethought

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Quick Ratio**
Ayo is **80 kg** on Earth. On Mars:
`80 × 0.38 = 30.4 kg (effective weight equivalent)`

**Example 2 — From Mass & g**
Mass `m = 65 kg`.
- Earth weight (N): `65 × 9.81 ≈ 637 N`
- Mars weight (N): `65 × 3.71 ≈ 241 N`
Ratio: `241 / 637 ≈ 0.378` → ~**38%**

Try it yourself in the calculator above and compare with Jupiter or the Moon for fun.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the calculator showing "mass" or "weight"?

We present an **Earth-scale equivalent** (what a bathroom scale would read if it were calibrated like Earth's). If you need **force in newtons**, use the mass×g method described above.

### Why do some tables show slightly different numbers?

Different sources round gravity values differently. We use widely accepted standard values and clearly state assumptions.

### Does clothing or gear change the reading?

Yes — anything you carry adds to **mass**, which scales the weight number on any world.
