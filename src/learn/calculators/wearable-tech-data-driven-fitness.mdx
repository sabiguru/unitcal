## What Counts as Wearable Tech?

Wearable fitness technology includes any device that collects physiological or movement data to guide healthier decisions.

### Common Types

- **Smartwatches & fitness trackers:** Monitor steps, heart rate, HRV, and sleep  
- **Smart rings & bands:** Provide continuous recovery and temperature data  
- **Smart apparel & sensors:** Analyse posture, breathing, or muscle activation  
- **Connected scales:** Track weight, body-fat %, and lean-mass trends  
- **AI-driven coaching apps:** Convert raw metrics into tailored plans  

According to the **American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)**, wearable tech remains the *#1 global fitness trend*, showing how deeply data-based training is shaping modern health habits.

  ## The Core Metrics to Track

  | Metric | What It Measures | Why It Matters |
  |---------|------------------|----------------|
  | **Resting Heart Rate (RHR)** | Cardiovascular fitness | Lower = better conditioning |
  | **Heart-Rate Variability (HRV)** | Nervous-system recovery | High = adaptable, resilient body |
  | **Sleep Score** | Quality & duration of rest | Drives muscle repair & hormone balance |
  | **VO₂ Max / Cardio Fitness Score** | Aerobic capacity | Predictor of endurance & longevity |
  | **Calories Burned / Active Minutes** | Energy expenditure | Sync with [Calorie Calculator](/calculator/calorie) |
  | **Steps / Movement Index** | Total daily activity | Prevents sedentary risk |
  | **Recovery Readiness Score** | Combined HRV + sleep + HR | Guides training intensity |

  ### How to Use Your Data Effectively  

  1. **Record a baseline:** Track 7 – 10 days before changing habits.  
  2. **Define clear goals:** Weight loss, muscle gain, or endurance.  
  3. **Correlate with calculators:**  
     - [BMI Calculator](/calculator/bmi) for body-size trends  
     - [Body Fat Calculator](/calculator/bodyfat) for composition  
     - [Calorie Calculator](/calculator/calorie) for nutrition balance  
  4. **Review weekly patterns** instead of obsessing over daily fluctuations.  
  5. **Train smarter:** Adjust effort using recovery scores—not mood alone.
