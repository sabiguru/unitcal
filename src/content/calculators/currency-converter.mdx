**Converted Amount = Original Amount × Live Exchange Rate**

### Why is my bank’s rate different?
Banks and money services add a **markup** (typically 2–5%) to the mid-market rate to make a profit. Our tool shows the baseline rate—your actual transfer will likely be slightly less due to these margins.

### What is the mid-market rate?
The **mid-market rate** is the midpoint between the buy and sell prices of two currencies in global markets. It’s the fairest rate and is used by financial institutions when trading among themselves.

### Can I use this for historical rates?
Yes. In the advanced view, you can select any date in the past 10 years to see historical exchange rates—useful for accounting or analysing trends.

### Does this include fees?
No. This tool shows **pure currency conversion** using live rates. It does not include bank fees, transfer fees, or foreign transaction charges. Always check total costs with your provider.

### How accurate is this converter?
It’s highly accurate for **informational purposes**. The rates match those used by financial professionals. However, consumer services rarely offer the exact mid-market rate.

### What’s the best way to get this rate in real life?
Use **specialist money transfer services** like Wise or Revolut, which offer rates very close to the mid-market with transparent fees. Avoid airport kiosks and high-street banks for large amounts.

### Can I convert cryptocurrencies?
Not in this tool. This converter is for **fiat currencies** only (e.g., GBP, USD, EUR). Cryptocurrency conversions require a different data source due to volatility and market fragmentation.
