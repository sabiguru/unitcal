## Understanding Fraction to Percent Conversion

A percentage is simply a fraction with a denominator of 100. Converting a fraction to a percentage means expressing it as parts per hundred.

**Formula:**
Percentage = (Numerator ÷ Denominator) × 100

**Why it works:**
- Fractions represent parts of a whole
- Percentages represent parts per hundred
- Dividing gives the decimal form, multiplying by 100 gives the percentage

---

## How to Convert Fractions to Percentages Manually

**Step 1:** Divide the numerator by the denominator
Example: 3/4 → 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75

**Step 2:** Multiply the result by 100
0.75 × 100 = 75

**Step 3:** Add the percent symbol
75%

**Alternative Method for Simple Fractions:**
Find an equivalent fraction with denominator 100
Example: 1/4 = ?/100
1/4 = 25/100 = 25%

## Common Fraction Conversions

**Halves and Quarters:**
- 1/2 = 50%
- 1/4 = 25%
- 3/4 = 75%

**Thirds:**
- 1/3 ≈ 33.33% (repeating)
- 2/3 ≈ 66.67% (repeating)

**Fifths:**
- 1/5 = 20%
- 2/5 = 40%
- 3/5 = 60%
- 4/5 = 80%

**Eighths:**
- 1/8 = 12.5%
- 3/8 = 37.5%
- 5/8 = 62.5%
- 7/8 = 87.5%

**Tenths:**
- 1/10 = 10%
- 3/10 = 30%
- 7/10 = 70%
- 9/10 = 90%

## FAQs

**Q: Can I convert improper fractions to percentages?**
A: Yes! Improper fractions (where numerator > denominator) will give percentages greater than 100%.

**Q: How do I handle repeating decimals?**
A: Some fractions like 1/3 produce repeating decimals (0.333...). Round to a reasonable number of decimal places.

**Q: What if my fraction has a decimal numerator or denominator?**
A: This calculator handles decimal inputs. For example, 1.5/2 = 75%.

**Q: Is 1/2 exactly 50%?**
A: Yes, some fractions convert to exact percentages, while others produce repeating or terminating decimals.

**Q: Can I convert mixed numbers?**
A: Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions first. For example, 1 1/2 = 3/2 = 150%.
