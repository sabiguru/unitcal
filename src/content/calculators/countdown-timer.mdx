Our **countdown timer** creates a live, real-time countdown to any future event or deadline. Whether you’re anticipating a wedding, launching a product, or tracking a personal goal, this tool displays the exact time remaining in days, hours, minutes, and seconds—and automatically adjusts for your time zone to ensure global accuracy.

### How It Works
The timer calculates the difference between the **current time** (from your device or a synced time server) and your **target date and time**. It then breaks this duration into readable units and updates every second for a live ticking effect.

Key features include:
- **Time zone awareness**: Set the event time in its local time zone (e.g., “New York”), and the timer shows the correct countdown for viewers worldwide.
- **Daylight Saving Time (DST) handling**: Automatically adjusts for DST changes using global time zone rules.
- **Shareable links**: Generate a unique URL to share your countdown with anyone.
- **Embed code**: Insert the timer directly into your website or blog.
- **Finale actions**: Play a sound, show a message, or trigger an animation when time runs out.

### How to Use
1. Enter your **event name** (e.g., “Product Launch”).
2. Set the **target date and time**.
3. Select the **correct time zone** (critical for accuracy).
4. Click **Create Timer**.
5. Use the **Share** button to send the link or **Embed** to add it to your site.

Always set the time zone to where the **event is happening**, not your current location—this ensures everyone counts down to the same global moment.

### Step-by-Step Example
**Scenario**: Countdown to a webinar on **10 July 2025 at 2:00 PM BST (London time)**.

**Steps**:
1. Event Name: “Q3 Strategy Webinar”
2. Date: 10 July 2025
3. Time: 14:00
4. Time Zone: `Europe/London`

**Result**:  
- A viewer in London sees: “3 days, 5 hours…”  
- A viewer in New York sees: “3 days, 0 hours…” (since it’s 9 AM there)  
- Both reach zero at the **exact same universal time**

**Use**: Share the link in your email invite so all attendees know precisely when to join.

### How do I set the correct time zone?
Always choose the time zone **where the event is taking place**. For example, if your wedding is in Paris, select `Europe/Paris`. The timer will then show the correct countdown for guests in Tokyo, Sydney, or Los Angeles.

### Can I embed this timer on my website?
Yes. After creating your timer, click **Embed** to get an HTML code snippet. Paste this into your website’s HTML to display the live countdown directly on your page.

### What happens when the timer reaches zero?
You can configure the finale: display a custom message (e.g., “It’s live!”), play a sound, show confetti, or redirect to a URL. By default, it stops ticking and shows “00:00:00:00”.

### Does it work on mobile phones?
Yes. The timer is fully responsive and works seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

### Is the timer accurate across time zones?
Yes. It uses Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) as a reference and applies IANA time zone rules, including Daylight Saving Time adjustments, so all users see the correct remaining time to the same global event.

### Can I create multiple timers?
Yes. You can create as many timers as you need—for different events, deadlines, or goals. Each has its own unique shareable link.

### What if I enter a past date?
The timer will show a negative countdown (e.g., “-2 days”) to indicate the event has already passed. You can edit or delete it.

### Do I need to keep the page open?
No. The countdown is based on absolute time, not your session. You can close the tab and reopen the link later—the timer will instantly show the correct remaining time.
