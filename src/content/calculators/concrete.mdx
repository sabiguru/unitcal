Our **concrete calculator** helps you estimate the exact volume of concrete needed for slabs, footings, columns, and more—saving you money and preventing project delays. Whether you’re ordering ready-mix by the cubic yard or using bagged concrete for small jobs, this tool ensures you get the right amount every time.

### How It Works
The calculator uses geometric formulas based on your project shape:
- **Slab/Footing**: `Length × Width × Thickness`
- **Column**: `π × Radius² × Height`
- **Stairs**: Sum of individual step volumes

All inputs are converted to **cubic feet**, then to **cubic yards** (÷27) for ready-mix orders. For bagged concrete, it estimates the number of 60-lb or 80-lb bags needed.

### Key Inputs
- **Shape**: Select slab, footing, column, or stairs.
- **Dimensions**: Enter length, width, thickness, diameter, or height.
- **Waste Factor**: Add 5–15% for spillage and uneven ground (recommended: 10%).
- **Bag Size**: Choose 60-lb or 80-lb for small projects.

The tool instantly shows:
- Volume in cubic feet and cubic yards
- Recommended order quantity (with waste)
- Number of concrete bags (if applicable)

### How to Use
1. Measure your formwork dimensions accurately.
2. Select the correct shape.
3. Enter all dimensions in feet or inches (the calculator handles conversion).
4. Add a waste factor (default: 10%).
5. Click **Calculate** to see your results.
6. Use the output to place your order with confidence.

Always double-check measurements—concrete can’t be returned!

### Step-by-Step Example: Patio Slab
**Project**: 15 ft × 12 ft patio, 4" thick.

**Inputs**:
- Shape: Slab
- Length: 15 ft
- Width: 12 ft
- Thickness: 4 in
- Waste: 10%

**Calculation**:
- Thickness in feet: `4 ÷ 12 = 0.333 ft`
- Volume: `15 × 12 × 0.333 = 60 ft³`
- Cubic yards: `60 ÷ 27 = 2.22 yd³`
- With waste: `2.22 × 1.10 = 2.44 yd³`

**Result**: Order **2.5 cubic yards** of ready-mix concrete.

For a small post hole (18" diameter, 24" deep), the calculator shows **3.5 ft³**, requiring **6 bags** of 80-lb concrete.

### How do I convert inches to feet for thickness?
Divide inches by 12. For example, 6" = 6 ÷ 12 = 0.5 ft. The calculator does this automatically if you select “inches.”

### What waste factor should I use?
Use **10%** for most projects. For complex shapes or uneven ground, use **15%**. For simple, well-formed slabs, **5%** may suffice.

### How many bags of concrete do I need?
- **60-lb bag** yields ~0.45 ft³  
- **80-lb bag** yields ~0.60 ft³  
The calculator divides your total volume by the bag yield and rounds up.

### Can I calculate stairs with this tool?
Yes. Enter the number of steps, tread depth, riser height, and width. The calculator sums the volume of all treads.

### What if my slab is on a slope?
Measure thickness at the high and low points, then use the **average**. For example, 4" and 6" → average = 5".

### Should I order extra concrete?
Yes. It’s cheaper to order 5–10% extra than to pay for a short-load delivery. The calculator includes this via the waste factor.

### How accurate is this calculator?
Very accurate—provided your measurements are correct. The formulas are standard engineering practice used by contractors.

### Do I need to account for rebar?
No. Rebar displaces less than 2% of concrete volume, which is ignored in standard estimates.
