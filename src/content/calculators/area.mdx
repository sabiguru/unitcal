## What is an Area Calculator?

An **area calculator** is a practical tool that instantly computes the surface area of common two-dimensional shapes. Whether you're a student working on geometry homework, a homeowner planning a DIY project, or a professional needing quick measurements, this calculator eliminates the need for manual formulas. Simply select the shape, input your dimensions, and get an accurate area measurement in square units like square feet (ft²) or square metres (m²). It's essential for tasks like ordering flooring, calculating paint coverage, or planning a garden layout.

## How Area Calculation Works

The calculator uses standard geometric formulas tailored to each shape. For a rectangle, it multiplies length by width. For a circle, it uses π (Pi) and the radius squared. The tool handles the maths behind the scenes, ensuring accuracy and saving you time. It can also manage unit conversions automatically, so you can input measurements in feet, metres, inches, or centimetres.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the difference between area and perimeter?**
A: **Area** measures the surface inside a shape (in square units like m²). **Perimeter** measures the total distance around the outside of the shape (in linear units like metres). You use area for covering a surface (e.g., with carpet) and perimeter for enclosing it (e.g., with a fence).

**Q: Can I calculate the area of an irregular shape with this calculator?**
A: The calculator is designed for standard shapes. For irregular shapes, the best approach is to divide the shape into multiple regular shapes (e.g., rectangles and triangles). Calculate the area of each part using this tool, and then add them together to get the total area.

**Q: How accurate is the calculator?**
A: The calculator is mathematically precise based on the formulas and the dimensions you provide. Its accuracy depends entirely on the accuracy of your measurements. Always double-check your measurements for real-world projects.

**Q: What units can I use?**
A: You can typically use any standard unit of length, such as feet, metres, inches, or centimetres. The calculator will return the area in the corresponding square units (ft², m², in², cm²). It can also convert between systems if you input measurements in different units.

**Q: How do I calculate the area of a circle if I only know the diameter?**
A: The calculator likely has a field for the diameter. If it only asks for the radius, remember that the radius is simply half of the diameter. So, if your diameter is 10 feet, your radius is 5 feet.

**Q: Why is it important to calculate area correctly for projects?**
A: An accurate area calculation ensures you buy the correct amount of materials, such as paint, flooring, or turf. Underestimating can cause project delays, while overestimating leads to wasted money and materials.

**Q: Does the calculator work for triangles where I don't know the height?**
A: The standard calculator requires the base and the perpendicular height. If you only know the lengths of the three sides (a SSS triangle), you would need to use Heron's formula, which may not be available in a basic calculator. Our tool focuses on the most common calculation methods.
