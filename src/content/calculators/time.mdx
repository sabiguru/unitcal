## Step-by-Step: Calculating Net Work Hours
**Scenario**: You clocked in at **8:47 AM** and out at **5:22 PM**, with a **40-minute unpaid lunch**.

1. **Calculate total time**:  
   - `17:22 – 08:47 = 8 hours 35 minutes`  
2. **Subtract lunch**:  
   - `8:35 – 0:40 = 7 hours 55 minutes`  
3. **Convert to decimal**:  
   - `7 + (55/60) ≈ 7.92 hours`

Use the calculator to input start/end times and breaks—it handles all conversions automatically!

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### How do I calculate time difference for a night shift?
If your shift crosses midnight (e.g., `10:00 PM` to `6:00 AM`), the calculator automatically adds 24 hours to the end time. Just enter the times as-is—the result will be **8 hours**.

### Can I add more than two time durations?
Yes! Use the “Add Duration” button to sum multiple entries (e.g., daily work logs for a week).

### Why convert to decimal hours?
Payroll systems require decimal hours (e.g., `7.75`), not `7:45`. The calculator provides both formats for flexibility.

### Does this work for seconds?
Yes—enter `HH:MM:SS` for second-level precision in scientific, sports, or technical applications.

### How do I handle AM/PM?
The calculator accepts both 12-hour (with AM/PM) and 24-hour formats. For time differences, 24-hour is recommended to avoid ambiguity.

### Can I multiply time by a number?
Yes—e.g., if a task takes `1:15`, enter `1:15 × 8` to get **10:00** total.

### Is this suitable for timesheets?
Absolutely. It’s designed for accurate payroll calculations, including break subtraction and decimal conversion.

### What if my total exceeds 24 hours?
No problem—the calculator displays totals like `25:30` (25 hours 30 minutes), ideal for weekly hour tracking.
