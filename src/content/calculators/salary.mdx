## How to Use the Salary Calculator
The **salary calculator** converts between hourly wages and yearly salaries, factors in overtime, bonuses, and commission, and breaks down your earnings by pay period (monthly, weekly, bi-weekly). Simply enter your base rate, working hours, and additional income, and the tool instantly shows your total gross compensation in all common formats. It’s perfect for evaluating job offers, planning a side hustle, or understanding your payslip—ensuring you never undervalue your work again.

### Key Formulas Used
- **Hourly to Annual**: `Hourly × Hours/Week × 52`
- **Overtime**: `Overtime Hours × Hourly × 1.5`
- **Pay Periods**: Annual ÷ 12 (monthly), ÷ 26 (bi-weekly), ÷ 52 (weekly)

### Best Practices for Accurate Results
1.  **Use your actual contracted hours**—not idealised full-time hours.
2.  **Include all income sources**: bonuses, commission, tips.
3.  **Specify pay frequency**—bi-weekly vs. semi-monthly changes your annual total.
4.  **For UK users**, remember that employer pension contributions are part of your total compensation.
5.  **Pair with a tax calculator** to estimate net (take-home) pay for budgeting.

## Step-by-Step Example
You’re offered a job at **£24/hour** for **35 hours/week**, with **5 hours of overtime weekly**.

**Step 1**: Enter hourly rate = £24, regular hours = 35  
**Step 2**: Add overtime = 5 hours at 1.5× rate  
**Step 3**: The calculator shows:  
- **Regular Weekly Pay**: 35 × 24 = **£840**  
- **Overtime Weekly Pay**: 5 × 36 = **£180**  
- **Total Weekly**: **£1,020**  
- **Annual Salary**: 1,020 × 52 = **£53,040**  
- **Monthly (bi-weekly)**: £2,040  

This gives you a complete picture to compare with salaried offers.

### How do I account for unpaid leave?
Reduce the “weeks per year” from 52 to your actual working weeks (e.g., 50 for 2 weeks unpaid holiday).

### Can this handle commission-based pay?
Yes—enter your base salary plus average monthly commission. The calculator will annualise the total.

### What if my overtime rate is 2× instead of 1.5×?
Adjust the overtime multiplier in the settings. Some roles (e.g., weekend work) pay double time.

### Is this accurate for UK tax calculations?
The calculator shows **gross pay**. For net pay, use our [UK Tax Calculator](/calculator/tax) which includes income tax, National Insurance, and pension deductions.

### How do I compare a salaried role to an hourly contract?
Convert both to annual gross income using this tool. Then add employer benefits (pension, healthcare) to the salaried role for a fair comparison.

### Does this include holiday pay?
Yes—if you’re paid hourly, holiday pay is included in your regular rate under UK law. The annual calculation assumes 52 weeks of pay.

### Can I calculate my effective hourly rate from a salary?
Yes—enter your annual salary and working hours. The tool will compute your hourly equivalent.

### What’s the difference between gross and net pay?
- **Gross pay**: Your total earnings before deductions  
- **Net pay**: Your take-home pay after tax, NI, and pension  
This calculator shows gross; use a tax calculator for net.
