## Calculator Guide

The Base Rate field accepts either hourly wage or annual salary, depending on the Input Type selected.

Weekly Hours determines the multiplier for hourly-to-annual conversion. Standard full-time is 40 hours (USA) or 37.5 hours (UK). Part-time workers should enter actual weekly hours.

Overtime Hours and Overtime Rate calculate additional compensation. Standard overtime is 1.5× (time-and-a-half); some industries pay 2× for weekends or holidays.

Additional Income captures bonuses, commissions, tips, or other variable compensation. Enter the expected annual total.

Results display:
- Annual salary equivalent
- Monthly, bi-weekly, weekly breakdown
- Hourly rate equivalent (including or excluding overtime)
- Overtime contribution to total compensation
- Total compensation including all additional income

**Standard Conversion Factors:**
- Annual to Monthly: ÷ 12
- Annual to Bi-weekly: ÷ 26
- Annual to Weekly: ÷ 52
- Annual to Hourly (40 hrs): ÷ 2,080
- Annual to Hourly (37.5 hrs): ÷ 1,950

## Salary Conversion Mathematics

### Hourly to Annual

**Formula:**
```
Annual = Hourly Rate × Hours/Week × 52 weeks
```

**Example: UK National Living Wage (April 2026)**
- Hourly Rate: £12.71
- Hours/Week: 40
- Annual: £12.71 × 40 × 52 = **£26,436.80**

This represents the annual earnings of a full-time worker on minimum wage.

### Annual to Hourly

**Formula:**
```
Hourly = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours/Week × 52)
```

**Example: UK Median Salary**
- Annual: £38,500
- Hours/Week: 37.5
- Hourly: £38,500 ÷ (37.5 × 52) = **£19.74/hour**

### With Overtime

**Formula:**
```
Regular = Hourly × Regular Hours × 52
Overtime = Hourly × Overtime Multiplier × Overtime Hours × 52
Total = Regular + Overtime
```

**Example: $25/hour with 5 hours weekly overtime at 1.5×**
- Regular: $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000
- Overtime: $25 × 1.5 × 5 × 52 = $9,750
- Total: **$61,750**

The 5 hours weekly overtime adds $9,750 annually—a significant 18.8% income boost.

## Contractor Versus Permanent Comparison

### The True Cost Comparison

A contractor day rate must cover benefits that permanent employees receive automatically:

| Component | Permanent Employee | Contractor |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| Base Salary | Included | Day rate |
| Pension (5% employer) | Provided | Self-funded |
| Holiday Pay (28 days) | Paid | Unpaid |
| Sick Pay | Statutory minimum | None |
| National Insurance (employer) | Paid by employer | Self-funded |
| Training/Development | Often provided | Self-funded |

### Conversion Formula

**Equivalent Day Rate = (Annual Salary × 1.3) ÷ 220**

The 1.3 multiplier accounts for benefits; 220 represents working days after holiday and sick leave.

**Example: £60,000 Permanent vs. Contractor**

Permanent Value:
- Base: £60,000
- Pension (5%): £3,000
- Holiday Pay: Built-in
- **Total Value:** ~£63,000+

Equivalent Day Rate:
- (£60,000 × 1.3) ÷ 220 = **£354.55/day**

A contractor must charge at least £355/day to match a £60,000 permanent role.

### IR35 Considerations (UK)

IR35 legislation affects contractor taxation. Inside IR35, contractors pay similar taxes to employees but without benefits—effectively the worst of both worlds. Outside IR35, contractors retain tax efficiency but face greater scrutiny.

### Beyond Base Salary

Total compensation includes:

| Component | Typical Value | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Base Salary | 70-80% of total | Core compensation |

**Offer A:**

Despite £5,000 lower base salary, Offer A provides nearly equivalent total compensation with better work-life balance.

## How This Calculator Works

**Hourly to Annual:**
```
regularAnnual = hourlyRate × hoursPerWeek × 52
overtimeAnnual = hourlyRate × overtimeMultiplier × overtimeHours × 52
totalAnnual = regularAnnual + overtimeAnnual + bonus + commission
```

**Annual to Hourly:**
```
hourlyRate = annualSalary / (hoursPerWeek × 52)
```

**Pay Period Conversion:**
```
monthly = annual / 12
biWeekly = annual / 26
weekly = annual / 52
daily = annual / (daysPerWeek × 52)
```

**Effective Hourly (including overtime):**
```
totalHours = (regularHours + overtimeHours) × 52
effectiveHourly = totalAnnual / totalHours
```

All calculations execute locally within the browser.

## FAQs

**What is the UK National Living Wage for 2026?**

From 1 April 2026, the National Living Wage increases to £12.71 per hour for workers aged 21 and over. This represents a 4.1% increase, adding approximately £900 to annual earnings for full-time workers. The rate for 18-20 year olds rises to £10.85 (8.5% increase).

**What is the US federal minimum wage in 2026?**

The federal minimum wage remains $7.25 per hour, unchanged since 2009. However, 23 states are increasing their minimum wages in 2026, with Washington leading at $17.13 and California at $16.90. Seventeen states plus DC will have $15+ minimum wage by 2026.

**How do I convert annual salary to hourly rate?**

Divide annual salary by total working hours. For 40-hour weeks: divide by 2,080. For 37.5-hour weeks (UK standard): divide by 1,950. Example: £45,000 ÷ 1,950 = £23.08 per hour.

**What are AI engineer salaries in 2026?**

AI/ML engineers command $140,000-$600,000+ depending on experience and company. Average base salary is approximately $195,000-$206,000. Total compensation at major tech companies reaches $451,000+, with Netflix posting roles up to $900,000.

**How does contractor rate compare to permanent salary?**

A useful formula: Equivalent Day Rate = (Annual Salary × 1.3) ÷ 220. The multiplier accounts for benefits (pension, holiday pay, sick leave). A £60,000 permanent role equates to approximately £355/day contracting rate.

**What is the median UK salary in 2026?**

The median gross weekly earnings for full-time UK employees reached £766.60 in April 2025, representing £39,863 annually. London significantly exceeds this at £46,800 median, whilst the North East lags at £32,960.

**What is the median US salary in 2026?**

The median annual wage in the US is approximately $62,192, with average wages around $70,000. Salaries vary significantly by state, ranging from $75,000+ in California and Connecticut to $45,000 in Mississippi.

**How should overtime be calculated?**

Standard overtime is 1.5× (time-and-a-half) the regular hourly rate. Some industries pay 2× for weekends or holidays. Calculate: Overtime Hours × Hourly Rate × Multiplier × 52 weeks for annual overtime income.

**How do I compare job offers with different benefits?**

Calculate total compensation: base salary + expected bonus + pension match value + healthcare value + holiday day value (daily rate × days). Two offers with different structures may have similar total value despite different base salaries.

**What industries pay the highest salaries?**

Technology (especially AI/ML), finance, healthcare (physicians), and legal services consistently rank highest. AI specialists currently command premiums of 30-50% above general tech roles. Healthcare professionals and corporate lawyers also earn well above median wages.
