## What is a Basic Calculator?

A **basic calculator** is a simple, reliable tool for performing everyday arithmetic. This free online calculator allows you to instantly add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers with a clean, intuitive interface. It's designed for quick calculations like balancing a chequebook, figuring out a tip, splitting a bill, or checking homework. With essential features like memory functions and a calculation history, it's the perfect **standard calculator** for students, professionals, and anyone who needs fast answers without the complexity of a scientific calculator.

## How It Works

Our **online calculator** uses core arithmetic principles to deliver accurate results. It follows the standard order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS), ensuring that expressions like `3 + 4 × 5` are calculated correctly as 23, not 35. The tool is powered by JavaScript, providing instant feedback without reloading the page, making it a fast and efficient **simple calculator** for your daily needs.


## How to Use This Calculator

Using this **free calculator** is straightforward. Follow these steps for quick and accurate results.

1.  **Enter Numbers:** Click the number buttons on the screen or use your keyboard's number pad.
2.  **Choose an Operation:** Press the function you need: plus (+), minus (-), multiply (×), or divide (÷).
3.  **Get Your Result:** Press the equals (=) button or hit 'Enter' on your keyboard to see the answer.
4.  **Use Memory for Complex Sums:** For multi-step problems, use the memory keys:
    *   `M+` to add a number to memory.
    *   `M-` to subtract from memory.
    *   `MR` to recall the stored number.
    *   `MC` to clear the memory.

### Pro Tip
You can use your keyboard for even faster input. The `*` key is for multiplication and the `/` key is for division. Press `Enter` to calculate.


## A Step-by-Step Example

**Scenario:** You're at a restaurant with a bill of £45.60. You want to leave a 15% tip and split the total equally between three people.

1.  **Calculate the Tip:** 
    *   Input: `45.60 × 15 %`
    *   The calculator shows the tip amount: **£6.84**

2.  **Find the Total Bill:**
    *   Simply press `+ 45.60 =` to add the tip to the original bill.
    *   The total is: **£52.44**

3.  **Split the Bill:**
    *   Press `÷ 3 =`
    *   The result is: **£17.48 per person**

**Final Result:** Each person should pay approximately **£17.48**. You can perform this entire calculation as a chain: `45.60 × 15 % + 45.60 ÷ 3 =`.

A: The **C (Clear)** button erases the last number you entered, letting you fix a mistake. The **AC (All Clear)** button completely resets the calculator, clearing the current operation and memory. Use AC to start a brand new calculation.
