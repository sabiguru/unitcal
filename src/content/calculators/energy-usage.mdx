<TabPanel title="Quick Guide">
Our **energy usage calculator** helps you estimate electricity consumption and costs for individual appliances or your entire home. By converting wattage ratings and usage patterns into kilowatt-hours (kWh) and pounds, this tool reveals which devices contribute most to your energy bill—so you can prioritise efficiency upgrades and reduce costs.

### How It Works
The calculator uses the standard energy formula:  
**`Energy (kWh) = Power (W) × Time (h) ÷ 1,000`**  
Then multiplies by your electricity rate to show costs.

For appliances that cycle on/off (fridges, AC), it applies a **duty cycle** (%) to estimate average power. You can also model **time-of-use tariffs** (e.g., Economy 7) by specifying different rates for peak/off-peak hours.

### Key Inputs
- **Appliance**: Select from common devices or enter custom wattage
- **Usage**: Hours per day and days per month
- **Duty Cycle**: For cycling appliances (default: 50% for fridges)
- **Electricity Rate**: Enter your £/kWh rate (check your bill)
- **Tariff Type**: Standard or time-of-use (ToU)

The tool instantly shows:
- Daily/monthly energy consumption (kWh)
- Monthly cost (£)
- Annual cost and CO₂ emissions
- Savings from efficiency upgrades

### How to Use
1. **For single appliances**: Enter wattage, usage, and rate to see costs.
2. **For whole-home audit**: Add multiple appliances to identify top energy users.
3. **For efficiency planning**: Compare old vs. new appliance costs to calculate payback periods.
4. **For tariff optimisation**: Model ToU rates to shift loads to cheaper periods.

Always use actual wattage from nameplates or energy monitors for best accuracy.

<TabPanel title="Example">
### Step-by-Step Example
**Scenario**: Calculating costs for a household fridge.

**Inputs**:
- Appliance: Refrigerator
- Nameplate Wattage: 200W
- Duty Cycle: 50% (runs half the time)
- Usage: 24 hours/day
- Electricity Rate: £0.28/kWh

**Calculation**:
- Average Power: `200W × 0.5 = 100W`
- Daily Energy: `(100W × 24h) ÷ 1,000 = 2.4 kWh`
- Monthly Cost: `2.4 kWh × 30 × £0.28 = **£20.16**`
- Annual Cost: **£242**

**Insight**: This fridge costs £242/year to run. Replacing it with a 100W model would save **£121/year**—paying for itself in 3–4 years.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### How do I find my appliance’s wattage?
Check the **nameplate** (sticker on the device), user manual, or manufacturer’s website. If unavailable, use our database of typical values—but for accuracy, measure with an energy monitor.

### What is a duty cycle?
**Duty cycle** is the percentage of time a cycling appliance (like a fridge or AC) actually runs. A 50% duty cycle means it’s on half the time. The calculator uses this to estimate average power consumption.

### Can I model time-of-use tariffs?
Yes. Select “Time-of-Use” tariff and enter separate rates for peak and off-peak hours. The calculator will show savings from shifting usage (e.g., running dishwasher overnight).

### How accurate is this calculator?
It’s highly accurate if you input correct wattage and usage. Nameplate ratings are often maximums—actual use may be 10–30% lower. For precise results, use a plug-in energy monitor.

### What are phantom loads?
**Phantom loads** are energy used by devices in standby mode (e.g., TVs, chargers). They typically add 5–10% to bills. Use our calculator to estimate these by entering always-on devices.

### How can I reduce my energy bill?
- Replace old appliances with efficient models (A+++ rating)
- Switch to LED lighting
- Use smart power strips to eliminate standby power
- Shift high-energy tasks to off-peak hours
- Lower thermostats by 1–2°C

### Does this include gas usage?
No. This calculator is for **electricity only**. For gas, use our [Home Energy Audit Calculator](/calculator/home-energy-audit).

### Can I estimate solar savings?
Yes. The calculator shows your total kWh usage—use this to size solar panels. A typical UK home uses 8–12 kWh/day, requiring a 3–5 kW solar system.
