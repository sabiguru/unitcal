## Introduction
Our **tile calculator** helps you estimate the number of tiles, thinset, and grout needed for any tiling project—floors, walls, or backsplashes. By accounting for **grout lines**, **waste factors**, and **layout complexity**, it ensures you order the right amount of materials from the start, avoiding costly mid-project shortages.

### How It Works
1. **Measures area** from your room dimensions
2. **Adjusts for grout lines** to calculate effective tile coverage
3. **Applies waste factor** based on your selected layout (straight, diagonal, herringbone)
4. **Estimates thinset and grout** using industry-standard coverage rates

### How to Use
1. Enter **room length and width** (metric or imperial)
2. Select **tile size** (or enter custom dimensions)
3. Choose **grout width** (e.g., 2 mm, 1/8")
4. Pick your **layout pattern** to set the waste factor
5. The tool shows:
   - Total area
   - Number of tiles needed (with waste)
   - Boxes required (based on common pack sizes)
   - Estimated thinset and grout quantities

Ideal for DIYers, tradespeople, and interior designers planning residential or commercial tiling projects.


## Step-by-Step Example
**Scenario**: You’re tiling a **2.4 m × 1.8 m bathroom floor** with **300 mm × 300 mm tiles**, using **3 mm grout lines** in a **straight lay pattern**.

**Steps**:
1. Enter Room Dimensions: `2.4 m` × `1.8 m`
2. Enter Tile Size: `300 mm` × `300 mm`
3. Select Grout Width: `3 mm`
4. Choose Layout: `Straight Lay` (10% waste)
5. The calculator computes:
   - Total Area: **4.32 m²**
   - Effective Tile Area: **0.0918 m²**
   - Base Tiles: **47**
   - Final Tiles (with 10% waste): **52**
   - Boxes (10 tiles/box): **6 boxes**
   - Thinset: **~14 kg** (3.2 kg/m² × 4.32 m²)
   - Grout: **~2.2 kg** (0.5 kg/m² × 4.32 m²)

✅ **Result**: Order **6 boxes of tiles**, **15 kg thinset**, and **2.5 kg grout**.


### Why does the calculator include grout lines?
Grout lines take up space—ignoring them causes under-ordering. A 300 mm tile with 3 mm grout covers **2% less area** than its face size suggests.

### How is the waste factor determined?
- **Straight Lay**: 10% (simple cuts)
- **Running Bond**: 15% (half-tile cuts)
- **Diagonal/Herringbone**: 20%+ (45° cuts on every tile)

### Can I use this for walls and backsplashes?
Yes. Just enter the **height** instead of room width for backsplashes, or total wall area for full-height tiling.

### What if my tiles are sold by the square metre?
The calculator shows **total m² needed** (including waste), so you can order directly by area.

### How accurate are the thinset and grout estimates?
They’re based on **industry averages**. For precise amounts, check your product’s coverage chart—porous tiles or uneven substrates may require more.

### Should I include the area under fixtures?
Yes. Always tile the **entire floor**, including under toilets and cabinets, in case they’re moved later.

### Can I calculate border or accent tiles?
Not directly. Calculate the main field first, then measure the **perimeter** separately for border tiles.

### What if my room has a bay window?
Break the room into **rectangles**, calculate each section, and sum the totals manually.
