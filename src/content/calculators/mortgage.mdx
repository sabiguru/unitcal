## How to Use the Mortgage Calculator
The **mortgage calculator** gives you a complete breakdown of your monthly home loan costs, including **Principal & Interest (P&I), taxes, insurance, PMI, and HOA fees**. Simply enter the home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term, and the tool instantly computes your total payment and generates a full **amortisation schedule**. It also models the impact of **extra payments**, helping you see how small changes can save thousands in interest and shorten your loan term.

### Key Formulas Used
- **P&I Payment**: `PMT = [P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ] / [(1 + r)ⁿ - 1]`
- **PMI**: `(Loan Amount × PMI Rate) / 12`
- **Escrow**: `(Annual Taxes + Annual Insurance) / 12`
- **Total Payment**: `P&I + Escrow + PMI + HOA`

### Best Practices for Accurate Results
1.  **Use realistic estimates** for taxes and insurance—check local council and insurer rates.
2.  **Include PMI** if your down payment is less than 20%.
3.  **Compare 15-year vs. 30-year terms** to see long-term savings.
4.  **Model extra payments** to find a comfortable overpayment strategy.
5.  **Get official quotes**—this is an estimate; your lender’s Loan Estimate is definitive.

You’re buying a **£280,000** home with a **15% down payment**.

**Step 1**: Enter values

**Step 3**: Add **£100 extra/month** → Loan paid off in **26 years**, saving **£36,000** in interest.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### What is PMI, and do I need it?
**PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance)** is required if your down payment is **less than 20%**. It protects the lender, not you. You can request removal once you reach **20% equity**.

### Why is my payment higher than the P&I?
Your total payment includes **escrow (taxes + insurance)** and **PMI** (if applicable). Lenders bundle these to ensure bills are paid.

### Can I use this for a UK mortgage?
Yes—for **repayment mortgages**. UK interest-only mortgages require a different calculation.

### How accurate is the amortisation schedule?
It’s mathematically precise for **fixed-rate loans**. Variable rates or fees will change actual payments.

### What if I make bi-weekly payments?
Bi-weekly payments (26 half-payments/year = 13 full payments) accelerate payoff. Use the **extra payment** feature to model this.

### Are closing costs included?
No. Closing costs are **upfront fees** (2–5% of loan). This calculator focuses on **monthly payments**.

### Should I pay points to lower my rate?
Use the calculator to compare **monthly savings** vs. **upfront cost**. If you’ll stay in the home longer than the break-even point, it may be worth it.
