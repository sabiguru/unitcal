## How to Use the Discount Calculator
The **discount calculator** instantly computes **sale prices, savings amounts, and discount percentages** for any shopping or pricing scenario. Simply enter the original price and discount percentage to get your sale price and total savings. It also handles **multiple sequential discounts**, **VAT-inclusive pricing**, and **bulk deal comparisons**—making it perfect for shoppers hunting deals, e-commerce sellers setting promotions, or businesses negotiating bulk orders. No more mental math or spreadsheet errors—just clear, accurate results in seconds.

### Key Formulas Used
- **Sale Price**: `Original × (1 – Discount%)`
- **Savings**: `Original – Sale Price`
- **Multiple Discounts**: `Original × (1 – D₁) × (1 – D₂)`
- **With VAT**: Apply discount pre-tax, then add 20% VAT (UK standard)

### Best Practices for Accurate Results
1.  **Enter pre-tax prices**—the calculator adds VAT after discounts (standard retail practice).
2.  **For multiple discounts**, enter them in the order applied (e.g., sale discount first, then loyalty discount).
3.  **Use exact percentages**—avoid rounding until the final result.
4.  **For bulk deals**, calculate per-unit price to compare fairly with % discounts.
5.  **Verify “was” prices**—retailers sometimes inflate originals to exaggerate savings.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### How do I calculate the discount percentage if I only know the original and sale price?
Use the formula: `[(Original – Sale) ÷ Original] × 100`. The calculator does this automatically—just enter both prices.

### Can this handle “Buy 2, Get 1 Free” deals?
Not directly—but you can calculate the effective discount:  
- Total items = 3, Paid items = 2 → Discount = (1/3) × 100 ≈ **33.3%**  
Enter 33.3% as the discount percentage to get the per-item sale price.

### Does this work for VAT-inclusive prices?
Yes—but specify whether the original price includes VAT. The calculator assumes **pre-VAT pricing** (standard for discounts). If your input includes VAT, select the “VAT-inclusive” option to adjust correctly.

### Why is my total with two discounts not 30% off?
Discounts are **multiplicative, not additive**. A 20% + 10% discount = 28% off:  
`1 – (0.80 × 0.90) = 0.28`. The calculator shows the true combined discount.

### Can I compare two different sale offers?
Yes! Calculate the **final price per unit** for each:  
- Offer A: 3 for £15 → £5/unit  
- Offer B: 25% off £7 → £5.25/unit  
The calculator helps you input these scenarios to see which saves more.

### Is this accurate for international sales tax?
The calculator uses **20% VAT** by default (UK standard). For other regions, manually adjust the tax rate or calculate pre-tax.

### How do I find the original price from a sale price?
Enter the sale price and discount percentage. The calculator reverses the formula: `Original = Sale ÷ (1 – Discount%)`.

### What if the discount is a fixed amount (e.g., £20 off)?
Select “Fixed Amount” mode. Enter the discount as a currency value instead of a percentage. The calculator will compute the effective % and final price.
