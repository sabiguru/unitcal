## How to Use the Roofing Calculator
The **roofing calculator** helps you estimate the exact materials needed for a new roof or re-roofing project. Simply enter your home’s **length and width**, select your **roof pitch**, and specify your **waste factor**, and the tool instantly calculates your **roofing squares, shingle bundles, underlayment rolls, and drip edge**. It accounts for the increased surface area due to roof slope, ensuring you never under-order materials again. Whether you’re getting quotes from contractors or planning a DIY project, this calculator gives you the precise quantities to budget accurately and avoid mid-project delays.

### Key Formulas Used
- **Actual Roof Area** = Footprint × Pitch Multiplier
- **Squares** = Actual Area ÷ 100
- **Bundles** = Squares × 3 (for standard shingles)
- **With Waste** = Squares × (1 + Waste Factor)

### Best Practices for Accurate Results
1.  **Measure your home’s footprint accurately**—include overhangs if significant.
2.  **Choose the correct pitch**—use a pitch gauge app or measure from the ground.
3.  **Use 10% waste for simple roofs**, 15–20% for complex roofs with hips/valleys.
4.  **Always round up squares**—suppliers sell in whole squares.
5.  **Add extra for repairs**—order at least 1 full square more than calculated.

## Step-by-Step Example
You’re re-roofing a **30 ft × 40 ft** house with a **6:12 pitch**.

**Step 1**: Enter dimensions → Footprint = 1,200 sq ft  
**Step 2**: Select pitch = 6:12 → Multiplier = 1.12  
**Step 3**: Actual Area = 1,200 × 1.12 = **1,344 sq ft**  
**Step 4**: Squares = 1,344 ÷ 100 = **13.44 → 14 squares**  
**Step 5**: Add 15% waste → 14 × 1.15 = **16.1 → 17 squares**  
**Step 6**: Results:
- **Shingle Bundles**: 17 × 3 = **51**
- **Underlayment**: 17 ÷ 4 = **5 rolls**
- **Drip Edge**: Measure eaves + rakes (e.g., 140 linear ft)

This tool turns complex geometry into a simple shopping list.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### How do I measure roof pitch from the ground?
Use a **smartphone pitch gauge app**, or:
1. Hold a level against the gable siding.
2. Mark 12 inches from the wall.
3. Measure vertically from that point to the roof edge.
4. This is your rise (e.g., 6 inches = 6:12 pitch).

### What if my roof has multiple sections?
Break your roof into simple rectangles or triangles. Calculate each section separately, then sum the squares.

### Can this calculator handle dormers or skylights?
It calculates the **main roof area**. For dormers, add their area manually. Skylights don’t significantly affect material needs—waste factor covers small penetrations.

### How accurate is the pitch multiplier?
Pitch multipliers are mathematically precise (based on Pythagoras). For a 6:12 roof: √(6² + 12²) / 12 = √180 / 12 ≈ 13.416 / 12 ≈ **1.118** → rounded to **1.12**.

### Do I need to include the garage?
Yes—if it’s under the same roof or you’re re-roofing it separately. Measure it as a distinct section.

### What’s the difference between 3-tab and architectural shingles?
Both cover 100 sq ft per square, but architectural shingles are heavier and may require **4 bundles per square**. Check the product specs.

### Can I use this for metal roofing?
No—metal roofing is sold by **panels or sheets**, not squares. Use a metal roofing calculator instead.

### Should I hire a professional to measure?
If your roof is complex (multiple hips, valleys, turrets), yes. For simple gable roofs, this calculator is highly accurate.
