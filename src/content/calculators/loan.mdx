Our **loan calculator** helps you estimate monthly payments, total interest, and payoff timelines for any fixed-rate loan. By inputting your loan amount, APR, and term, you’ll see a complete amortization schedule and discover how extra payments can save you money and shorten your debt term. Whether you’re financing a car, consolidating debt, or planning a major purchase, this tool provides the clarity to borrow wisely and repay efficiently.

### How It Works
The calculator uses the standard amortization formula:  
**`PMT = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n – 1]`**  
where **P** = principal, **r** = monthly rate (APR/12), and **n** = total payments.

It then generates:
- **Monthly payment**: Fixed amount for the loan term
- **Total interest**: Sum of all interest paid
- **Amortization schedule**: Breakdown of principal vs. interest per payment
- **Extra payment impact**: Shows time and interest saved with additional payments

### Key Features
- **Extra payment modeling**: Add monthly or one-time payments to see savings
- **Term comparison**: Adjust loan length to compare total costs
- **Visual charts**: See how principal and interest change over time
- **Mobile-friendly**: Works on any device for on-the-go planning

### How to Use
1. Enter your **loan amount** (e.g., £15,000 for a car)
2. Input the **APR** (e.g., 6%)
3. Select the **loan term** in months or years
4. Click **Calculate** to see your payment and schedule
5. Use the **extra payment** feature to explore payoff acceleration

This tool is ideal for comparing loan offers, budgeting for debt, or planning early payoff strategies.

### Step-by-Step Example
**Scenario**: £12,000 personal loan at 7% APR for 4 years.

**Inputs**:
- Loan Amount: £12,000
- APR: 7%
- Term: 48 months

**Calculation**:
1. **Monthly Payment**:  
   `r = 0.07/12 = 0.005833`  
   `PMT = [12000 × 0.005833 × (1.005833)^48] / [(1.005833)^48 – 1] = **£287.34**`
2. **Total Repayment**: `£287.34 × 48 = **£13,792.32**`
3. **Total Interest**: `£13,792.32 – £12,000 = **£1,792.32**`

**Extra Payment Insight**:  
Adding £40/month reduces payoff time to **38 months** and saves **£320** in interest.

### How accurate is this calculator?
It uses the exact amortization formula used by lenders, so results are mathematically precise for fixed-rate loans. Actual payments may vary slightly due to rounding or lender-specific rules.

### Can I model extra payments?
Yes. Add monthly or one-time extra payments to see how they reduce your loan term and total interest. The calculator shows exact savings.

### What’s the difference between APR and interest rate?
**APR** includes fees (origination, etc.), while the **interest rate** is just the cost of borrowing. Always compare APRs when shopping for loans.

### Should I choose a shorter loan term?
Yes—if you can afford the higher payment. A shorter term (e.g., 3 years vs. 5 years) drastically reduces total interest, often by 30–50%.

### Does this work for mortgages?
Yes—for the principal/interest portion. Mortgages often include taxes and insurance, which this calculator doesn’t model. Use our [Mortgage Calculator](/calculator/mortgage) for full estimates.

### How do extra payments save money?
They reduce your principal immediately, which lowers all future interest calculations. Even small extra payments create a snowball effect over time.

### Can I pay off my loan early?
Most personal and auto loans allow early payoff without penalties. Always confirm with your lender, as some mortgages charge prepayment fees.

### Is this calculator secure?
Yes. All calculations happen in your browser—no data is sent to our servers or stored.
