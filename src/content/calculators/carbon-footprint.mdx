<TabPanel title="Quick Guide">
Our **carbon footprint calculator** helps you estimate your annual CO₂ emissions from home energy, transport, diet, and consumption. Understanding your footprint is the first step toward reducing your environmental impact and aligning with UK net-zero goals.

### How It Works
The calculator uses official UK emission factors to convert your inputs into **tonnes of CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e)** per year:
- **Home**: Electricity (kWh) and gas (kWh or m³)
- **Transport**: Car mileage, flight distances, and public transport use
- **Diet**: Food preferences (high meat, vegetarian, etc.)
- **Goods**: Estimated spending on non-essential items

The formula is simple:  
**Emissions = Activity × Emission Factor**  
For example, driving 10,000 km in a petrol car emits `10,000 × 0.171 = 1,710 kg CO₂e`.

### How to Use
1. Enter your **annual electricity and gas usage** (from bills).
2. Add **car mileage**, **flight distances**, and public transport use.
3. Select your **diet type**.
4. Estimate annual **spending on goods**.
5. Click **Calculate** to see your total footprint and category breakdown.
6. Review **personalised tips** to reduce emissions.

The tool follows DEFRA and DESNZ guidelines and updates emission factors annually.

### Step-by-Step Example
**User Profile**:  
- Electricity: 3,500 kWh  
- Gas: 14,000 kWh  
- Petrol car: 12,000 km/year  
- One return flight to Italy: 2,800 km  
- Vegetarian diet  
- £4,000 annual goods spending

**Calculation**:
- **Home**: `(3,500 × 0.233) + (14,000 × 0.184) = 816 + 2,576 = 3,392 kg`
- **Transport**: `(12,000 × 0.171) + (2,800 × 0.154) = 2,052 + 431 = 2,483 kg`
- **Diet**: `1,800 kg` (vegetarian average)
- **Goods**: `4,000 × 0.5 = 2,000 kg`

**Total**: `3,392 + 2,483 + 1,800 + 2,000 = 9,675 kg CO₂e` (**9.7 tonnes**)

This is slightly below the UK average (~10–12 tonnes). The biggest reduction opportunities are reducing car use or switching to an EV.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### What is CO₂e?
CO₂e stands for **carbon dioxide equivalent**. It’s a standard unit that includes CO₂ plus other greenhouse gases (like methane) converted to their CO₂ warming impact.

### How accurate is this calculator?
It uses the latest UK government emission factors and is accurate for personal benchmarking. It won’t capture every detail (e.g., your exact car model), but it reliably shows which areas contribute most to your footprint.

### Can I include renewable energy?
Yes! If you’re on a **green electricity tariff**, the calculator reduces your electricity emissions by up to 100%, as per Ofgem guidelines.

### What’s a good carbon footprint?
The current UK average is **10–12 tonnes per person**. To meet the Paris Agreement, we need to reach **under 2 tonnes by 2050**. Start by cutting 1–2 tonnes through high-impact actions.

### How can I reduce my footprint?
Top actions:  
- **Fly less** or choose trains  
- **Switch to an electric vehicle** or use public transport  
- **Improve home insulation** and use a green energy supplier  
- **Eat less red meat**  
- **Buy fewer new goods**

### Is my data saved?
No. All calculations happen in your browser. We don’t store or track your inputs.
