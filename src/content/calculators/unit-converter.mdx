## Introduction
Our **unit converter** instantly transforms measurements between **metric and imperial systems**—from length and weight to temperature, volume, and speed. Whether you’re cooking, travelling, studying, or working on a DIY project, this tool ensures accurate, hassle-free conversions with a single click.

### How It Works
1. **Select a category** (e.g., Length, Weight, Temperature)
2. **Choose your “from” and “to” units** (e.g., miles → kilometres)
3. **Enter a value**—the tool instantly displays the converted result
4. **Supports compound units** like speed (mph ↔ km/h) and density

### Key Features
- **Comprehensive coverage**: 50+ units across 10+ categories
- **Exact conversion factors**: Uses internationally agreed standards (e.g., 1 inch = 2.54 cm)
- **Bidirectional**: Convert A→B or B→A with one toggle
- **Mobile-friendly**: Works seamlessly on any device

Ideal for students, chefs, engineers, and travellers who need reliable, instant conversions.


## Step-by-Step Example
**Scenario**: You’re driving in Europe and see a speed limit of **90 km/h**. What is that in miles per hour?

**Steps**:
1. Select category: **Speed**
2. Set “From”: `km/h`, “To”: `mph`
3. Enter value: `90`
4. The calculator instantly shows: **55.92 mph**

✅ **Result**: **90 km/h ≈ 56 mph**

This helps you stay within legal limits while driving abroad.


### Does it handle US vs. UK gallons?
Yes. The converter distinguishes between:
- **US gallon** (3.785 L)
- **UK (imperial) gallon** (4.546 L)  
Select the correct one based on your region.

### Can I convert temperature?
Yes. Supports:
- Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit
- Celsius ↔ Kelvin
- Fahrenheit ↔ Kelvin  
Uses exact formulas: `°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32`

### How accurate are the conversions?
All conversions use **internationally standardised factors** (e.g., NIST, ISO). Results are precise to 6+ decimal places—more than sufficient for everyday use.

### Can I convert area or volume?
Yes. Includes:
- **Area**: square feet ↔ square metres, acres ↔ hectares
- **Volume**: litres ↔ gallons, cubic feet ↔ cubic metres  
Automatically applies squared/cubed conversion factors.

### What if my unit isn’t listed?
The converter covers the most common units. If you need a rare unit (e.g., furlongs, stones), use the **dimensional analysis method** described in our Learn guide.

### Is this suitable for scientific work?
Yes—for most educational and engineering purposes. For high-precision scientific research, always verify with primary standards.

### Can I convert cooking measurements?
Yes. Includes:
- Cups ↔ millilitres
- Ounces ↔ grams
- Tablespoons ↔ millilitres  
Note: For dry ingredients, weight (grams) is more accurate than volume (cups).
