## How to Use the Password Generator
The **password generator** creates cryptographically strong, random passwords or memorable passphrases to secure your online accounts. Choose your preferred style—traditional (mix of letters, numbers, symbols) or passphrase (random words)—and customise length, character sets, and separators. Every password is generated **locally in your browser**, ensuring privacy and security. Use it to create unique credentials for every service, eliminating the risk of credential-stuffing attacks after data breaches.

### Key Features
- **Traditional Passwords**: Up to 128 characters with upper/lowercase, digits, and symbols
- **Passphrases**: 3–10 random words with hyphens, spaces, or no separator
- **Entropy Display**: See the strength in bits for every generated password
- **Copy to Clipboard**: One-click copy for easy use

### Best Practices
1.  **Never reuse passwords**—generate a new one for each account.
2.  **Use a password manager** to store generated passwords securely.
3.  **Enable 2FA** on all critical accounts (email, banking, social media).
4.  For your **password manager’s master password**, use a 5–6 word passphrase.

<TabPanel title="FAQ">
### Is this generator safe to use?
Yes. All passwords are created **locally in your web browser**—no data is sent to any server. However, only use it on a trusted, malware-free device.

### What is entropy, and why does it matter?
Entropy measures password strength in **bits**. Higher entropy = more guesses needed to crack it. A password with >80 bits is considered secure against brute-force attacks.

### Should I use a passphrase or a random password?
- **Passphrase**: Best for your **master password** (e.g., for a password manager)—memorable and strong.
- **Random password**: Best for **all other accounts**—maximises entropy and is managed by your password manager.

### Can I trust online password generators?
Only if they are **open-source and client-side** (like this one). Avoid generators that require you to “download” or “email” your password.

### What if a website has password rules?
Adjust the generator settings:
- Reduce length if capped
- Disable symbols if not allowed
- Always maximise length and character variety within the site’s limits.

### Do I really need a password manager?
Yes. Remembering dozens of unique, complex passwords is impossible for humans. A password manager makes strong security effortless.

### How often should I generate new passwords?
Only when:
- Creating a new account
- A service you use reports a breach
- You suspect your password was exposed  
Don’t rotate passwords unnecessarily—focus on uniqueness and strength.
